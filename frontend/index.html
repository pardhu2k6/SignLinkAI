About SignLink AI

SignLink AI turns hand signs into text and speech in real time. MediaPipe tracks 21 hand landmarks, a pretrained neural network classifies each pose in under a millisecond, and a temporal stabiliser turns noisy frames into clean, confirmed signs.

Built for accessibility — to help people with hearing or speech impairments communicate, and to help anyone learn sign language.
